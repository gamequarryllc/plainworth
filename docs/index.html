Plainworth

A private net worth and monthly spending tracker for iPhone. No sign-up, no bank linking, nothing uploaded.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: gamequarryllc@protonmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

How do I move my data to a new iPhone? In Settings, tap Back Up Now and save the file to iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install Plainworth and tap Restore from Backup.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back? Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Plainworth Pro sheet.

Can you see my numbers? No. They're stored only on your phone. See the privacy policy.
