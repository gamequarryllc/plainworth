Plainworth Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Plainworth is a net worth and monthly spending tracker made by Game Quarry LLC. It's built so that your financial information stays with you.

Your data stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Plainworth (accounts, balances, debts, spending and income) is stored only on your device, unless you turn on iCloud sync (below). Plainworth has no servers and no accounts, doesn't link to your bank, and never uploads, sells or shares your data. We can't see it.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

Sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings. While it's off, Plainworth doesn't use iCloud at all. If you turn it on, the data you enter is also kept in your own private iCloud storage, under your Apple ID, so your other devices can show the same data. Apple stores it and moves it between your devices under Apple's privacy policy. It never passes through us, and we can't see it. You can turn sync off at any time, and choose to delete your data from iCloud when you do.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export to Excel, Plainworth creates a file on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example iCloud Drive). That file is then handled by the service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

Metal prices

If you track gold, silver, platinum or palladium, Plainworth fetches current market prices from gold-api.com. The request only asks for a price and contains none of your data. Like any website, gold-api.com can see your device's IP address.

Purchases

Plainworth Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. Plainworth checks that you own Pro with Apple's own system on your device; no other company is involved. Restoring Pro on a new device works through your Apple ID with Apple.

No tracking or ads

Plainworth contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

Plainworth doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase All Data, or delete the app. If you never turned on iCloud sync, nothing is stored anywhere else, so that removes it completely. If sync is on, Erase All Data also erases it from iCloud and your other devices; or turn sync off and choose Delete from iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
