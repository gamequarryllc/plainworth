Plainworth Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Plainworth is a net worth and monthly spending tracker made by Game Quarry LLC. It's built so that your financial information stays with you.

Your data stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Plainworth (accounts, balances, debts, spending and income) is stored only on your device. Plainworth has no servers and no accounts, doesn't link to your bank, and never uploads, sells or shares your data. We can't see it.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export to Excel, Plainworth creates a file on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example iCloud Drive). That file is then handled by the service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

Metal prices

If you track gold, silver, platinum or palladium, Plainworth fetches current market prices from gold-api.com. The request only asks for a price and contains none of your data. Like any website, gold-api.com can see your device's IP address.

Purchases

Plainworth Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. To confirm that you own Pro and to restore it on a new device, Plainworth uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and a random identifier created by the app. It doesn't receive your name, email address or any data you enter in Plainworth, and it's not used for advertising or tracking.

Face ID

Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. Plainworth never has access to your face data.

No tracking or ads

Plainworth contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

Plainworth doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase All Data, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that removes it completely.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
